# Terms

Placekeepers is free to use. It is built for taking care of your community and acting lawfully,
not for anything else.

**This is not legal advice**, and Placekeepers is not affiliated with the City of Philadelphia or
any other government agency. For anything that depends on being right, such as who owns a
property or whether a legal route applies to you, confirm it with the City office that holds the
record, or with a lawyer. [How to do it](https://holdthedoorhoid.github.io/placekeepers/how/)
points to free legal help.

## How to use this site

Please do not use Placekeepers to harass anyone, to target the property of someone who has died,
or to speculate, meaning buying land cheaply just to resell it for a profit. The site is built to
help neighbors and organizers care for a place, and we ask that you use it the same way.

## About the facts on the map

The map republishes public records from the City of Philadelphia and other public sources, listed
with their license on our Data status page. Public records can be wrong, incomplete, or out of
date. Before you act on anything important, such as who owns a property or how much is owed on
it, check with the City office responsible for that record.

If you find a mistake, please tell us through
[Contact](https://holdthedoorhoid.github.io/placekeepers/contact/). If you want your own
property's record corrected, we will point you to the City office that keeps that record, since we
only republish what the City already makes public.

## No promises

We do our best to keep Placekeepers accurate and working, but we cannot promise it is always
right, complete, or available. Use your own judgment, and check anything important before you
rely on it.
