# Contact

## Report a correction

If something on the map looks wrong, a lot's details, a legal route, or anything else, please
tell us with this short form. It already asks for the place and what looks wrong, so we can check
it quickly.

[Report a correction](https://github.com/holdTheDoorHoid/placekeepers/issues/new?template=correction.yml)

## Suggest a data source

If you know of a public data source Philadelphia neighbors would find useful, tell us about it
here.

[Suggest a data source](https://github.com/holdTheDoorHoid/placekeepers/issues/new?template=data-source.yml)

## Tell us about a project you finished

If neighbors finished a cleanup, a garden, a place to sit, a mural, trees, or got a bench, a
shelter or a 311 fix from the City, tell us with this form. A person reads every report, and once
it is approved it appears on the map under "Projects neighbors finished", with a link back to the
report. The map never shows who sent it.

[Tell us about a project you finished](https://github.com/holdTheDoorHoid/placekeepers/issues/new?template=project.yml)

## A bus stop, shelter or bench is missing or wrong

What our map says about benches, and about shelters the City's own list does not have, comes from
OpenStreetMap, the free map anyone can improve, so the fastest fix is to correct OpenStreetMap
itself. The City's list of bus shelters was last changed in January 2025; where it and
OpenStreetMap disagree, the stop's page says so, and a survey settles it. Our guide to
[surveying bus stops](../streetcomplete/) shows how, with a free phone app. Your change reaches our
map within about a week.

## Ask for a memorial to come down

Anyone can ask for a memorial marker, or a name, to be taken off the map, no questions asked and
no proof needed. {{REMOVAL_EMAIL}}

Once we take a memorial down, we keep a record of its id only, never a name, so it does not come
back in a later update.

## Everything else

For anything that does not fit the forms above, open a general issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/holdTheDoorHoid/placekeepers/issues/new/choose), or use the
correction form and explain what you need in the description.
