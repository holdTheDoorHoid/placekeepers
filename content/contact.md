# Contact

## Report a correction

If something on the map looks wrong, a lot's details, a legal route, or anything else, please
tell us with this short form. It already asks for the place and what looks wrong, so we can check
it quickly.

[Report a correction](https://github.com/holdTheDoorHoid/placekeepers/issues/new?template=correction.yml)

## Suggest a data source

If you know of a public data source Philadelphia neighbors would find useful, tell us about it
here.

[Suggest a data source](https://github.com/holdTheDoorHoid/placekeepers/issues/new?template=data-source.yml)

## A bus stop, shelter or bench is missing or wrong

Bus stops, shelters and benches on our map come from OpenStreetMap, the free map anyone can
improve, so the fastest fix is to correct OpenStreetMap itself. Our guide to
[surveying bus stops](../streetcomplete/) shows how, with a free phone app. Your change reaches our
map within about a week.

## Ask for a memorial name to come down

Anyone can ask for a name to be removed from the map, no questions asked. {{REMOVAL_EMAIL}}

We do not use a public form for this, because a removal request is personal and private. Once we
take a name down, we keep a record of the parcel or crash id only, never the name, so it does not
come back in a later update.

## Everything else

For anything that does not fit the two forms above, open a general issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/holdTheDoorHoid/placekeepers/issues/new/choose), or use the
correction form and explain what you need in the description.
