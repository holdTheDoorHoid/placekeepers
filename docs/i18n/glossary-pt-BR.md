# Brazilian Portuguese glossary (pt-BR)

The key terms of Placekeepers in Brazilian Portuguese, written once so the interface catalog
(`web/src/i18n/locales/pt-BR.ts`), the registry text (`registry/i18n/pt-BR.yaml`) and the content
pages (`content/pt-BR/`) all use the same word. A reviewer who speaks Portuguese can read this one
page to judge the choices.

## Voice

- Plain, friendly Brazilian Portuguese. The reader is "você", with the imperative in the "você"
  form ("Escolha", "Toque", "Confira"). Most Portuguese speakers in Philadelphia are Brazilian.
- Short sentences, everyday words, no legal or academic jargon where a plain word exists.
- No dashes as punctuation (the tests enforce it). Ranges are written "de 2019 a 2024". Hyphens
  inside compound words and names are fine.
- Money keeps the dollar: "US$ 6.500". Numbers follow pt-BR ("1.234,5"); the site formats them.
  Measures stay in US units where the source gives them (pés, milhas, polegadas, galões), since
  the signs and the City's records use them.
- Gender neutral where easy: "Morreu ao caminhar" (died while walking), "proprietário" as the
  general word for owner.

## Names that stay as they are

Placekeepers, Philly311, 311, PHS LandCare, PHS Community LandCare, SEPTA, Fraud Guard, Tangled
Title Fund, Philadelphia VIP, Garden Justice Legal Initiative, Grounded in Philly, Urban Garden
Agreement, Community Garden License, Neighborhood Gardens Trust, Families for Safe Streets,
StreetComplete, OpenStreetMap, Wikidata, Mapping Inequality, Little Free Library, TreePhilly, PHDC,
L&I, OPA, EPA, FEMA, PennDOT, PECO, DVRPC, Vision Zero, Percent for Art, Atlas, Tax Center, Land
Bank.

Programs the Land Bank runs keep their English names. The Land Bank itself is written "Land Bank",
the way it writes its own name (phillylandbank.org); phila.gov's Portuguese page calls it "Banco de
terras da Filadélfia", and the longer pages give that once in parentheses.

## Plurals

Portuguese counts 0 and 1 as the same plural form in the standard rules, but people write
"0 lugares". The catalog's `pl()` helper gives zero the plural form.
