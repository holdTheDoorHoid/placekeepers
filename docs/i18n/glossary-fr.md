# French glossary (fr)

The words chosen for the French translation of Placekeepers (issue #49, part B), so the interface
(`web/src/i18n/locales/fr.ts`), the registry text (`registry/i18n/fr.yaml`) and the content pages
(`content/fr/`) use one word for one idea. A reviewer who changes a term here should change it in
all three places. English is the source of every word.

## Voice and typography

| Choice | Rule |
|---|---|
| Register | Plain international French, "vous", short sentences, no regionalisms, readable by neighbors from West and Central Africa and Haiti as well as from France and Quebec. |
| Gender | The generic masculine for people in general ("les voisins", "les organisateurs"); "une personne" where English says "someone". |
| Spaces | A narrow no break space (U+202F) before `:` `;` `!` `?` `%` and `$` after a number, inside « guillemets », and as the thousands separator ("24 166", "6 500 $"). Telephone numbers keep ordinary spaces ("215 686 9200"). |
| Quotes | « guillemets » for quoted words and for the names of layers and settings. Apostrophes stay straight (as in the English source). |
| Numbers | Decimal comma ("0,2 pour cent", "1,5 fois"). Percent is written "61 %" in figures and "pour cent" in running sentences where English spells it out. |
| Units | The site's own units stay: feet, miles, inches, gallons. The tree watering line adds the liters ("20 gallons, environ 75 litres"). Distances in meters stay meters. |
| Dashes | None. A colon, a comma, parentheses or a new sentence take their place, as everywhere on the site. |
| Link text | Links to the site's own pages are relative (`../how/`), as `docs/TRANSLATING.md` asks, and the link words are the French menu names. |

## Words that need care

| Word | Choice |
|---|---|
| "dangerous" | Never used for a place or a neighborhood. It appears only in the HOLC's own 1937 grade D wording (« dangereux », in quotes, as a record of the agency's judgment) and for the L&I status "imminently dangerous" (« en danger imminent »). The L&I status "unsafe" is « non sûr ». |
| "clean", "safe" (soil) | The brownfield note never says the soil is clean or safe: « Testez le sol avant de cultiver des aliments. » |
| "died" (owner) | The possible estate text says « Il se peut que le propriétaire enregistré soit décédé », never that the owner died. |
| police, tickets, enforcement | Never suggested. The only mentions are quotations of what the map does not do ("Ce n'est pas un outil de police"). |
| "token" and "nominal" prices | « prix symbolique », never "prix nominal". |
