# Khmer glossary (km)

The words chosen for Placekeepers in Khmer, so the interface, the registry text and the content
pages say the same thing the same way. English is the source. Written by the translating agent,
not yet checked by a native speaker.

## Style

- Standard written Khmer, plain and polite. "អ្នក" for "you". "យើងខ្ញុំ" for "we" (polite).
- Sentences end with the Khmer full stop "។". The Khmer colon "៖" is used where English uses a colon.
  Commas are used sparingly (Khmer separates phrases with spaces); no comma at all in the notes
  that go at the top of a CSV file, as in English.
- No dashes as punctuation. Digits stay Western (0 to 9), as the site formats them.
- A zero width space (U+200B) sits between words, inserted by running every Khmer run through the
  ICU Khmer word segmenter (`Intl.Segmenter('km')`), so lines break between words. The Khmer
  files in the repository carry them; edit a copy with them removed and put them back.
- Latin script is kept for proper names and for words a neighbor will see in English on the
  City's own pages (see "Names that stay in English").

## What the City itself writes in Khmer (phila.gov, "I speak" card of 2021)

- City of Philadelphia: "ទីក្រុង Philadelphia" (the English name stays in Latin script).
- City agency: "ទីភ្នាក់ងារក្នុងក្រុង"; City government: "រដ្ឋាភិបាលទីក្រុង"; services: "សេវាកម្ម";
  information: "ព័ត៌មាន"; residents: "ពលរដ្ឋ"; free: "ដោយឥតគិតថ្លៃ".
- phila.gov publishes no Khmer names for L&I, Philly311, the Land Bank, PHDC, the Redevelopment
  Authority or their programs, so those stay in English.
