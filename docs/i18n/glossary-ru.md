# Russian glossary (ru)

The key terms of the Russian translation, fixed once so the interface catalog
(`web/src/i18n/locales/ru.ts`), the registry text (`registry/i18n/ru.yaml`) and the content pages
(`content/ru/`) say the same thing the same way. Add to this list before you change a term.

Register: standard, plain, polite Russian, addressing the reader as "вы". Short sentences. No
dashes as punctuation: a comma, a colon, brackets or a new sentence instead of the Russian
dash. Quotation marks are guillemets, «like this». Decimal comma, and a no break space in numbers
from five digits ("10 465"); four digit numbers and money are written without a space ("$6500").
Plural forms in `plural()` use `one`, `few`, `many` and `other` (the last for fractions, which take
the genitive singular: "1,5 мили").

## The "Did it work?" page (M5.4)

| English | Russian | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Did it work? | Сработало ли это? | the menu item and the page title; the lot page's "whether care worked" is "сработал ли уход" |
| care (done to a place: cleaned, greened, sealed, demolished) | уход | "places that got care" is "места, получившие уход"; never alone after "после", because "после ухода" reads as "after leaving": always "после ухода за местами". "Забота" stays for the abstract "where care is needed most" |
| comparison, comparison places | сравнение, места для сравнения | "similar places that did not get care" is "похожие места, где ухода не было" |
| verdict labels | «Меньше после ухода за местами», «Больше после ухода за местами», «Явных изменений нет», «Мы не смогли определить», «Слишком мало, чтобы судить» | "We could not tell" stays a plain admission, never "нет эффекта" |
| "This does not show that care caused them." | «Это не показывает, что причиной стал уход за этими местами.» | kept on every "more afterwards" verdict |
| the real evidence | настоящие доказательства | the randomized trials; the records "can mislead" is "может ввести в заблуждение" |
| 90 percent interval | 90-процентный интервал | a change in words: "3,2% больше", "6,2% меньше", "без изменений"; a range is "от 6,2% меньше до 0,2% больше" |

## Phrases that must match the ETHICS passages

The six word for word passages keep the same Russian wherever they appear (catalog, registry and
the Use this responsibly page quote them exactly):

- possible estate: «Владелец, указанный в официальных записях, мог умереть. …»
- conservatorship warning: «Консерваторство (conservatorship) может лишить собственника его недвижимости. …»
- deed fraud notice: `dossier.owner.deedFraud`
- greening caution: «Озеленение может поднять цены по соседству. Подумайте о том, чтобы сочетать его с мерами защиты.»
- gardens, seating and art caution: `displacement.placemakingCaution`
- brownfield soil note: «… Сделайте анализ почвы, прежде чем выращивать продукты питания.»

## What the plumbing leaves in English inside Russian sentences

- Times of day on a stop's page ("6:50 AM") come from code, not from the catalog
  (`web/src/transit/describe.ts`, `clock()`).
- A route survey's duration and distance ("about 1 hour 15 minutes", "0.4 miles") come from code
  (`web/src/survey/sheet.ts`, `formatDuration()` and `formatMiles()`).
- The words the City publishes in its own data: owner names, violation titles, board names in
  appeals, zoning codes, SEPTA stop names, period labels such as a ridership season.
