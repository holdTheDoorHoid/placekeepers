# Placekeepers v0.1

Placekeepers is a free, public map that helps Philadelphia neighbors and organizers find vacant
lots and dangerous streets, and the lawful way to do something about them. This is our first
public release.

**Try it:** https://holdthedoorhoid.github.io/placekeepers/

## Highlights

- **Find vacant land.** About 82,000 lots and buildings with a sign of vacancy get a full page:
  confidence, who owns it, sale history, and the first lawful step to get permission.
- **What you can do nearby.** On a phone, search an address or tap "Near me" for the closest
  places that could use care, each with its best suggestion and legal first step.
- **Compare and plan.** On a desktop, blend a violence reduction lens and a street safety lens,
  filter, sort a ranked list, and download the results.
- **Street safety and memorials.** The High Injury Network, years of crash records, and a quiet
  marker for each person the Police record as killed while walking, cycling or riding a scooter,
  now in its own list too.
- **An early look at transit.** SEPTA's bus, trolley and train stops and routes (how often
  service comes, how many people ride) and which stops OpenStreetMap knows have a shelter or a
  bench, off by default under Buses and trains. The transit comfort lens and its suggestions
  come in version 0.2.
- **Built for everyone.** Every screen passes automated accessibility checks, phones keep more
  room for the map, and the site is tested to talk only to its own server and the City's.
- **Careful with sensitive facts.** Owner flags such as "possible estate" are written to protect
  families, never to help anyone target a home. See
  [Use this responsibly](https://holdthedoorhoid.github.io/placekeepers/responsibly/).

## Not in this release yet

Names of people killed: we are asking the Bicycle Coalition and Families for Safe Streets for
their blessing first. Removal requests go through a public GitHub issue for now; a private email
address is planned. The transit comfort lens and its suggestions, heat and shade, public art,
history, and organizing tools are planned for later releases.

## Known limits

- About 400 parcels (398 as of October 4, 2026) lack full sale and inspection history; their
  lot pages say so and offer a live lookup instead.
- No filters yet by council district, community organization, neighborhood or zoning.
- Street blocks and crash sites still open only from the map; a list for them, like the one
  memorials now have, is planned.
- The analysis view's map can take several seconds to finish drawing on a busy computer.
