# Placekeepers v0.2

Placekeepers v0.2 adds a first look at transit comfort: SEPTA's bus and trolley stops, scored for
where a shelter, a bench or shade would help riders most, plus printable survey tools. It also
carries an early look at two later releases: heat and shade, and amenities around the city.

**Try it:** https://holdthedoorhoid.github.io/placekeepers/

## Highlights

- **Transit comfort.** Every SEPTA bus and trolley stop and route in Philadelphia, scored by
  riders, a missing shelter or bench, little shade, crossing the High Injury Network, and long
  waits, each with a "why" breakdown and its first lawful step.
- **Help map the stops.** The "Survey bus stops" guide for the free StreetComplete phone app, and
  a new "Survey a route" kit: a printable sheet for every SEPTA route and direction, in order.
- **An early look at heat and shade.** A lens for where planting trees or greening a lot would
  cool people most: neighborhood heat risk, tree canopy, and City street trees nearby.
- **An early look at amenities.** Benches, water, toilets and little free libraries from
  OpenStreetMap; Free Library branches, recreation centers and pools from the City; and 311
  reports of dumping, dark streetlights and graffiti by block.
- **Faster, and honest about samples.** The whole city view loads a lighter copy of the lots on
  phones, and the analysis view says plainly when its list, plot and downloads show only a
  sample, not every place.
- **Careful with licenses.** OpenStreetMap's shelter and bench answers are joined to SEPTA's
  stops in your browser, not stored together in our files, so each source's license stays with
  its own data.

Heat and shade already has its own lens and suggestions; amenities and 311 conditions are real
data with no lens or suggestions yet. Both are Phase 3 work, ahead of schedule. Public art,
walkability, history and organizing tools are still ahead.

## Known limits

- Only about 1 in 10 bus and trolley stops are known to OpenStreetMap so far (829 of 7,927), so
  most stop pages say "not yet surveyed" for a shelter or bench.
- No confirmed public way to request a bus shelter yet: OTIS picks stops itself, under its
  advertising contract.
- A lot page opened from a link or a search, rather than by tapping the map, does not yet show
  its score breakdown.
- Street blocks and crash sites still open only from the map; a list for them is planned.
- No filters yet by council district, community organization, neighborhood or zoning.

## How to help

Survey a bus or trolley stop with the free StreetComplete app; see the "Survey bus stops" guide
on the site. Tell us what is wrong or missing on the
[Contact page](https://holdthedoorhoid.github.io/placekeepers/contact/) or a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/holdTheDoorHoid/placekeepers/issues/new/choose).
